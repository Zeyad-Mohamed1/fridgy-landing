Works in English and Egyptian Arabic

Sara stops at the supermarket

On her way home from work, Sara picks up a few things: milk, eggs, vegetables and chicken.

She gets home and opens the fridge to put everything away. That's when she sees she already had milk and eggs, and a bag of vegetables she'd forgotten about since last week.

Some of it had to go in the bin

The worst part? Some of those vegetables had already gone bad and had to be thrown away.

It isn't the first time this has happened to Sara, and it won't be the last.

So why does it keep happening?

The food we forget

The problem isn't only that we buy too much. It's that we don't always remember what we have, what's about to go off, and what we actually need.

  • "What are we eating today?"
  • "Do we have any tomatoes?"
  • "Is the milk still good?"

The same questions, every single day.

Food waste starts at home

Sara isn't alone. Most of the food the world throws away is thrown away in our own kitchens.

Why it keeps happening

  • We forget what's inside. So we buy milk and eggs we already have.
  • We can't see what's expiring. Vegetables go bad at the back of the shelf.
  • Tracking feels like admin. Spreadsheets and long forms get abandoned in a week.

Source: UNEP Food Waste Index Report 2024

You talk to Fridgy, and it keeps everything organized.

Who Fridgy is for

We're starting with urban households in Egypt, in English and Egyptian Arabic. These are the people who feel the problem most.

  • Busy professionals Like Sara. They shop on the way home, have no time to plan, and forget what's already in the fridge.
  • Families and shared homes Several people shop for one fridge, so the same things get bought twice. Fridgy keeps one shared list for the whole household.
  • Health and fitness focused They train several times a week, follow a diet or avoid allergens, and want meals that fit their goals.
  • Students and young couples A first kitchen and a tight grocery budget. Every wasted item hurts, and they're still learning what to cook.

How Fridgy makes money

Fridgy is free to start, so every household can stop wasting food. Revenue grows as people plan, shop and cook through the app.
